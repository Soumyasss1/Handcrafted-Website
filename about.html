About Us

Crafting beauty with love, passion, and creativity.

Who We Are

HandCrafted is a home for unique, handmade products created with passion by talented artisans. Every item is carefully designed, ensuring the perfect blend of tradition, creativity, and authenticity. Our mission is to bring handcrafted beauty into every home.

Our Mission

We believe handmade products carry emotions, stories, and soul. Our goal is to support local artisans and deliver meaningful creations to customers who value originality.

Meet the Founder

Soumya Srivastava

Founder & Creative Designer

With a strong love for handmade crafts and creativity, Soumya started HandCrafted to bring together artisans and people who appreciate the magic of handcrafted products.
